# Risk and Safety Guidance

ClimateOps provides environmental information and decision support. It is not an emergency service, an official warning authority, or a substitute for local instructions from public authorities.

## User-Facing Safety Requirements

- Show the affected location and the observation or forecast time.
- Show source, freshness, and relevant uncertainty.
- Explain that conditions can change rapidly.
- Direct users to official authorities for urgent decisions, evacuation orders, medical needs, and emergency assistance.
- Avoid language that guarantees safety, accuracy, or outcomes.
- Use plain severity labels and explain what they mean.

## Hazard-Specific Considerations

- **Heat:** Encourage official heat-health guidance and account for vulnerable people, workers, and access to cooling.
- **Flooding:** Never imply that a low model score means an area is safe; local drainage, terrain, and rapid rainfall can change conditions.
- **Drought and water stress:** Distinguish environmental indicators from official restrictions and avoid unsupported claims about water availability.
- **Water emergencies:** Identify whether information is observed, reported, estimated, or awaiting verification.

## Failure and Uncertainty

When data is stale, missing, contradictory, or outside validated coverage:

1. Say what is unavailable.
2. Show the last known time and source when safe to do so.
3. Avoid converting uncertainty into a low-risk result.
4. Provide a retry or alternative-source path.
5. Refer urgent decisions to official channels.

## Change Review

Any change to risk thresholds, alert wording, recommendations, source ranking, or incident status should include:

- The rationale and evidence.
- Affected hazards and user groups.
- False-positive and false-negative considerations.
- Manual scenarios covering severe and missing-data cases.
- Documentation and rollback notes.

## Privacy

Collect the minimum location and account information needed for the stated purpose. Do not expose precise user locations, contact details, or incident reports beyond their intended audience.
