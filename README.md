# 🌍 ClimateOps
### Real-Time Environmental Risk Monitoring & Insights

ClimateOps is an environmental emergency monitoring platform
built for the Environmental Hacks hackathon by WeMakeDevs.

The project focuses on helping users understand environmental
risks associated with floods, droughts, heatwaves, and water
emergencies through location-based risk information and
actionable insights.

## 🌱 About the Project

Environmental emergencies can affect communities, infrastructure,
agriculture, and daily life. Timely access to environmental
information can help people make better-informed decisions.

ClimateOps aims to bring environmental data and risk insights
together in one platform, helping users understand potential
hazards relevant to their locations.

## 🚨 Problem Statement

Environmental risks such as floods, droughts, heatwaves, and
water emergencies can create serious challenges when useful
information is difficult to access or interpret.

There is a need for accessible, location-based environmental
insights that can support awareness and informed decision-making.

## 💡 Our Approach

ClimateOps focuses on using environmental data to provide
location-based risk information and actionable insights.

The platform is organized into frontend and backend components,
supporting the development of an integrated environmental
monitoring solution.

## 🎯 Key Objectives

- Support awareness of environmental hazards.
- Present location-based environmental risk information.
- Make environmental insights easier to understand.
- Help users make informed decisions during potential emergencies.
- Build a foundation for future environmental monitoring features.

## ✨ Key Features

- Environmental emergency monitoring concept
- Location-based risk insights
- Focus on floods, droughts, heatwaves, and water emergencies
- Actionable environmental information
- Modular frontend and backend project structure
